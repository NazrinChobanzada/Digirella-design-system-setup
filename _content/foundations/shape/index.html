Shape & border

tokens/shape.css · Buttons are rectangular at --radius-control (4px) — an action reads as a control, not a token. Chips, tags and compact steppers stay fully pill-shaped, which is what separates a filter you toggle from an action you commit. Containers use 10px. Borders are 1px and low-contrast — prefer space over rules.

TokenValueUsage
--radius-xs3pxBadge, small checkbox
--radius-sm6pxInput, notification
--radius-md10pxCard, panel
--radius-lg14pxModal, hero
--radius-xl20pxLarge media block
--radius-control4pxButton — actions read as controls
--radius-pill999pxChip, tag, stepper, search field
