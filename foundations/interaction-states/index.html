Interaction states

Five rules cover the whole system; a component that needs a sixth is doing too much. Every component section below carries its own state matrix, rendered side by side so states can be compared without hovering.

StateRuleTokens
hoverOne step darker, or a neutral fill on transparent controls. Never an opacity change.--color-primary-hover · --surface-hover
activeOne step darker again. No scale, no translate — pressing must not move the target.--color-primary-active · --surface-active
focus2px green ring at 2px offset, identical on every focusable element including destructive ones. Fields use a 3px tinted halo instead, because a ring around a 38px box reads as an error.--focus-ring · --border-focus
selectedFilled with primary green where the control is the choice (Tag, Pagination); tinted green where the row merely holds it (table row, option).--color-primary · --surface-brand-subtle
disabledNeutral fill, --text-disabled, cursor: not-allowed. Colour is dropped entirely — a faded green still reads as available.--surface-disabled · --text-disabled

Timing is --duration-fast on --ease-standard for all five. Focus is the exception: it appears instantly, because a delayed focus ring feels broken to keyboard users.
